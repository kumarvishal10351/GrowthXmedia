FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Transparent answers.

Most agencies operate in silos: media buyers don’t speak with creative directors, and developers don’t understand marketing psychology. GrowthXmedia combines all three into a synchronized flywheel. We don’t just run ads; we build the creatives, code the landing pages, and automate the conversational chat funnels (WhatsApp & Telegram) that convert and retain customers.

We primarily operate on monthly strategic growth retainers starting at a 3-month initial commitment to allow for full diagnostic, build, and algorithmic scaling cycles. Our Phase 01 Diagnostic and initial Creative/Technical Sprint typically takes 14 to 21 business days. By the end of Week 3, campaigns, conversion pages, and automated sequences are live and collecting real market feedback.
